## Ask

The sales certification track has no stable body of knowledge. A third of its exam questions cite marketing strings on the website, which have already drifted; others cite private growth notes that a partner cannot read. The study modules and the enablement pages are two parallel copies of the same sales content and disagree in places. On top of that foundation the batch asks for three learning upgrades: a sales practice pool so the daily drill stops quizzing sellers on Docker sockets (#59), an objection-roleplay widget so the track trains the conversation rather than recall about it (#60), and diagram hotspots for the two structure-shaped sales topics (#61).

One plan, four phases, in issue order: H (handbook, #57) first, because the drill pool, the roleplay scenarios and the diagrams should cite the handbook rather than inherit the same rot. No existing plan covers the partner track (grep for exam-question-bank, practice-questions, roleplay, hotspot and handbook over agent/plans and agent/plans/_done on 2026-11-16; PLAN-backup-program.md H13 only fixed one technical question).

## Design

### H. The handbook (#57)

- **Where it lives: in the portal, not on www.** The enablement pages become the partner handbook: one canonical set of section exports under `web/src/data/enablement-content/`, each heading carrying a stable anchor id. The study modules import the factual sections they teach instead of restating them, and keep their own framing and widgets. Reasons over a public www docs page: (a) the verbatim-quote test can run inside the account repo against the handbook source, so the rot class is closed by a test rather than by care; (b) www docs pages carry a 13-locale translation duty and every English edit re-stales 12 locales, while study and enablement bodies are deliberately English-only and outside the i18n gates (`study-content/index.ts:34-35`); (c) sanitized battlecard and playbook material is partner-facing sales guidance, not public marketing.
- **The URL rule changes deliberately.** Sales questions take `sourceUrl` `/account/partner/enablement/<slug>#<anchor>` and `sourceDoc` `private/account/web/src/data/enablement-content/<file>.ts#<anchor>`; `exam-bank.ts` requires that form for `track: sales` and keeps `https://www.rediacc.com/` for technical and clustering. All 50 sales questions move, including the 12 that cite www docs, so one rule covers the whole track.
- **Content rules kept.** No invented customer, quote, logo or result; every number carries its source inside the handbook; trade-offs are stated. Growth material enters only as rewritten, sourced prose. Pricing follows the catalog (per-machine rate on 5-hour-window concurrent machines, floating licenses free after 5 hours), which resolves the ms5 contradiction.
- **The DB follows by migration.** One `UPDATE exam_questions SET source_url = ...` per sales id. Questions whose wording must change because the fact changed (at least the eight sales-pricing questions, reviewed against the catalog) also update prompt, choices or explanation and bump `revision`, the 0064 precedent; the parity test's sampled `revision === 1` assertion becomes a per-id expected revision.

### D. Sales drill (#59)

- `practice-questions-sales.ts` holds 40 sales questions: solutions 10, pricing 6, compliance 6, product-services 5, competitive 5, objections 4, use-cases 4 (TAXONOMY proportions rounded to 40); easy 16 / medium 18 / hard 6; correctIndex 10 each; ids `practice-sales-`. Each is answerable from the handbook and tests its mechanism from a different angle than any exam question.
- `Drill.tsx` gets a two-way track switch. The default is the track with more completed study modules (`partnerApi.study.getProgress`), and the choice persists in `localStorage`. The streak stays track-agnostic: one drill a day on either track counts.

### R. Objection roleplay (#60)

- New block `{ type: 'roleplay'; id; title?; intro?; steps: RoleplayStep[] }`, `RoleplayStep = { id; buyer: string; options: { text: string; explanation: string }[]; correctIndex: number }`. Structured text only, never markdown (the `types.ts:1026` rule). `ObjectionRoleplay.tsx` steps through the scenario like `TerminalSim.tsx`, reveals every option's explanation after a pick, and reports an attempt on the first pick. `roleplay` joins `INTERACTIVE_TYPES` and the `InteractiveBlock` union.
- Content: ms7 (Veeam incumbent, renewal-price objection) and ms8 (customer-reference ask, first discovery turn), built from the handbook's objection and positioning sections and the spot-mistake scenarios PR #64 shipped.

### G. Sales diagrams (#61)

- `ms7-competitive--market-map.svg` (self-hosted versus hosted, broad versus Linux-focused, one hotspot per quadrant with the honest trade-off) and `ms3-solution-stories--story-shape.svg` (pain, promise, mechanism, proof).
- `ms5-pricing--license-lifecycle.svg` shows only the floating-license mechanics: consumed on create or copy, free after 5 hours idle, daily work uses none. No plan name, edition or price in any SVG.

### Out of scope

The www marketing copy that the 17 questions used to cite is PLAN-www-funnel-truthfulness.md. Server-side per-track streaks are not built. Discovery-call simulations for other tracks reuse the roleplay block later and are not planned here.

## Risks

- Moving `sourceUrl` off www is a schema exception; if the operator prefers a public partner handbook on www, H3 publishes it as a docs page with 13 locales and the schema stays. Open question for approval, DEFAULT: the portal handbook as designed.
- Pricing questions are the most likely to need new wording; a revision bump changes a question mid-certification for candidates with an attempt in progress. H4 lands outside an active exam window if the exam service binds attempts to revisions.
- A sanitized battlecard can still overstate a competitor; every competitive claim in H3 carries a public source or is cut.
- The account surface needs a VERIFIED `npm run vm:check -- --changed` on the head tree before the live recorder runs (docs/agent-reference/plan-records.md, "The ladder").
