## Ask

The operator asked that logs survive a compromise of the software inside a repo (F1). They ship to Rediacc's R2 through create-only presigned URLs minted from the licence blob, every 30 seconds, signed and chained, with heartbeats, into a bucket locked from a Rediacc account no customer admin reaches, compacted nightly (F5). Only paid plans record, only while paid (F6). The quota is 25% of the backup quota (F7), evicted oldest first outside a locked newest 7 days (F8), raised only by an upgrade or, for ENTERPRISE, by a Rediacc admin (F9).

## Design

D1. **Batch format** (packages/shared/src/forensics/batch.ts, mirrored in Go). PLAN-forensics-capture.md D12 cuts a batch every 30 seconds, empty ones included so the batch doubles as the heartbeat, as one signed blob `{schema, machine_id, seq, prev_hash, cut_at, records[]}`.
A whole-blob signature cannot serve a per-repo day file or a 30-day address redaction, so the layout is a signed header plus sections, built into PLAN-forensics-capture.md FC5 from the start: header `{schema, machine_id, seq, prev_hash, cut_at, level, sections: [{repo_guid, kind: records|pii, sha256, bytes}]}`, each section zstd-compressed on its own, the header Ed25519-signed with the spool key; batch hash = SHA-256 of header bytes plus signature; `prev_hash` chains as before, `genesis` for the first. Detector `finding` records of PLAN-forensics-detection.md ride the records section, so they are signed at detection time. Known-answer vectors live in `__fixtures__/batch-vectors.json`; renet's test reads the same file by relative path, as schema_test.go does.

D2. **Session** `POST /forensics/session`, unauthenticated until verified, rate limited per address like the backup session: body `{licenses: SignedRepoLicense[] (1..64), machineId, machinePublicKey}`. Every blob must verify, be live, share one subscription and carry an enabled claim (D9). The subscription must be paid and not a trial (F6), or the answer is 403 `FORENSICS_NOT_ENTITLED`. The first session pins `machinePublicKey` per (subscription, machine); a different key is refused until an org owner re-pins it from the portal, audited. The answer: a session token (one hour, hashed at rest), the bound repo set, per-repo `{level, maxLevel}`, a `levelCap` from D6, `renew: [repoGuid]` for licences whose claim is stale, and the quota state.

D3. **Batch grant** `POST /forensics/batches` with `X-Forensics-Session`: body `{header, signature, sha256, bytes}`. The server verifies the signature against the pinned key, checks every section's repo is in the session, the level is within the claim (or a spike to Full, F3), and `bytes` at most 4 MiB. A `prevHash` other than the stored last hash or a skipped `seq` is accepted and recorded as a gap on the machine and in the next day header: a hidden gap would be worse evidence than a visible one. It reserves the bytes (D6), then answers one presigned PUT for `f/<sub>/<machine>/raw/<yyyy-mm-dd>/<seq>-<sha256>.batch`, create-only, size and checksum bound, one hour, signed with `ACCOUNT_FORENSICS_S3_*`.

D4. **The bucket and its lock.** One `forensics-<region>` bucket per region, with the region's jurisdiction, in a separate Cloudflare account for evidence held by Rediacc admins only. Lock rules: `raw/` and `days/` 7 days, `hold/` 400 days (PLAN-forensics-investigation.md copies an incident window there). Lifecycle: `raw/` deleted after 8 days, `tmp/` after 2 days. The Worker holds an Object Read and Write token for those buckets only, so neither it nor a customer admin can lift a lock (03-external-research.md: a bucket-admin token can). No `[[r2_buckets]]` binding is possible across accounts; the toml carries the endpoint and bucket as vars and the keys as secrets.
On-premise uses its own S3 store; its retention control is documented by PLAN-forensics-docs-legal.md.

D5. **Compaction.** The hourly cron enqueues one message per machine-hour on a `forensics-compact-<region>` queue; the consumer concatenates that hour's raw batches verbatim into `tmp/<sub>/<machine>/<day>/<hh>.part`. The 03:00 cron enqueues one message per machine-day, which writes `days/<sub>/<machine>/<day>/headers.ndjson.gz` (every signed header, gaps marked) and one `repo-<guid>.ndjson.gz` plus `repo-<guid>.pii.ndjson.gz` per repo. Line 1 of `headers` is `{v, machine, day, prevDay, prevSha256, repoFiles: {guid: sha256}, gaps}`, chaining the previous day's headers file. Part files are deleted after the day file is read back and its hash stored.
`private/account/scripts/forensics-verify.ts` re-verifies signatures and both chains through a read-only token. `forensics-reader.ts` is the batch reader PLAN-forensics-detection.md D7 walks: new raw batches per machine after a cursor, each fetched, its signature, section hashes and chain checked before a record is returned. Node and on-premise run the same consumer from a timer.

D6. **Quota and eviction.** `forensicsQuotaBytes` in PLAN_LIMITS: FREE 0, COMMUNITY 0, PROFESSIONAL 25 GiB, BUSINESS 125 GiB, ENTERPRISE 512 GiB, a quarter of `storageQuotaBytes` (F7). `subscriptions.forensics_quota_bytes` overrides it and is accepted only for ENTERPRISE, only on the elevated root route (F9).
`forensics_usage` holds `raw_bytes`, `day_bytes` and `hold_bytes` per subscription, separate from backup accounting: a grant adds the reservation, the hourly job reconciles a reservation whose object never landed (recorded as a gap), compaction adds day bytes, the nightly job drops raw bytes past 8 days, eviction subtracts. Locked bytes are raw, day files younger than 7 days and holds. Guards, in order: when the trailing 7-day ingest projected into the locked window exceeds the quota, `levelCap` drops to Basic; when Basic still does not fit, grants answer 507 `FORENSICS_QUOTA_LOCKED`; otherwise a grant over quota enqueues FIFO eviction of the oldest unlocked day files across the subscription.
A `forensics_quota` notice goes to owners and admins at 80% (warning) and at a downgrade or refusal (critical).

D7. **Paid only.** A subscription that is not paid, or is a trial, gets no session; renet stops capture and drops its unshipped spool when the session answers `FORENSICS_NOT_ENTITLED` or the claim says disabled, so nothing is recorded while unpaid and an upgrade starts empty (F6). Stored objects of a lapsed subscription age out under FIFO and the lifecycle rules.

D8. **GDPR.** The records section carries an inbound client address truncated to /24 or /48; the full address sits in the `pii` section.
The nightly job deletes `repo-<guid>.pii.ndjson.gz` past 30 days; the header still holds its hash, so the verifier reports `pii redacted` instead of a broken chain. Outbound destinations stay. The DPA wording is PLAN-forensics-docs-legal.md's.

D9. **Licence claim.** `RepoLicense.forensics?: {enabled, level, maxLevel}`, stamped at issue and renewal from entitlement and `forensics_repo_settings`; `maxLevel` is `full` on every paid plan, since F2 leaves the trade to the customer and D6 bounds the cost. The signer refuses an enabled claim on an unpaid plan. renet reads it through `license.ForensicsClaim`; a level change reaches the machine within the hour through `renew` in D2, which triggers a renewal of that licence.

D10. **Console.** `PUT /portal/forensics/repos/:guid/level` (owner or admin, the GUID must belong to the org's subscription through `repoLicenseIssuanceSlots`, private/account/src/db/schema.ts:659), `GET /portal/forensics/status` (per machine: last batch, silence past 90 seconds, gaps; usage and quota). The level card sits on the console repo page.

## Risks

- **D1 write rate.** One grant per machine every 30 seconds writes two rows; one D1 batch statement keeps it one round trip, and PLAN-d1-scaling.md's shard router carries the tables.
- **Doubled recent storage.** Raw batches and day files both exist for the newest 7 days; D6 counts both, so the locked-window guard sees the real cost.
- **A compromised host signs lies going forward.** It cannot rewrite batches already stored; the server detector of PLAN-forensics-detection.md and the gap records are the answer to forged or missing future batches.
- **An unlocked lag.** Objects are locked from creation, but a host cut off before shipping loses its unshipped spool if the disk is wiped; heartbeat silence makes that visible.
