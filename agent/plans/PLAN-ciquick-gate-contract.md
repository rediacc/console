## Design

**The contract.** Every registered gate declares three things in scripts/ci-runner/manifest.ts, regenerated into gates.lock.json:
1. Inputs: `paths`, `lists` or `names` (scripts/ci-runner/input-hash.ts:14-18), or `paths: ['**']` with a `BLOCKER:` reason. A whole-tree gate is listed in `.ci/policy/gate-contract-baseline.json`, seeded with today's 108 and shrink-only: a new entry fails, a drained entry fails until the baseline is rewritten, and every drain diffs old against new and asserts the added side is empty.
2. A reuse kind: `input` (the default for a carriable gate), or `clock|network|history|cache` from carry-exempt.json, which every exempt row and every `env` gate must carry.
3. A time budget: a fast gate inherits the 10 s tier (scripts/gates/check-gate-manifest.ts:91 `BUDGET_MS`); a `slow: true` gate declares `budgetS`. A gate whose p90 (lane-durations.json `gate_step_p90_seconds`, else .ci/cache/gate-durations.json, as check-gate-manifest.ts already reads) exceeds its budget is a finding, held in the same baseline file under a separate key.

**Under-declared paths are the real risk.** A gate whose `paths` miss a read carries a green it never judged. The trace already exists: scripts/ci-runner/path-trace.ts writes scripts/ci-runner/trace-ledger.ts, and scripts/ci-runner/trace-honest.ts fails a traced gate that reads an undeclared path. PLAN-ciquick-cpu.md W3.2 (honest declared sets) and W3.3 (wire the planned paths-honesty gate) are still open; C1 carries both, and the lead ticks them in PLAN-ciquick-cpu.md with C1's landed sha. scripts/gates/check-paths-origin.ts stays the provenance check for `pathsOrigin`.

**Three enforcement points, so another session cannot forget:** the gate (C1) in ci:quick and in the CI quality lane, a pre-edit guard (C2) at the moment a gate is added, and a Stop-hook duty (C3) that turns growth into owed work.

**The gate-author brief contradicts the contract today:** .claude/agents/gate-author.md says "Do not add `paths:` to a manifest entry to make it cheaper". D1 replaces that line with the contract and the trace control.
