## Context

1. **No delegation-cert expiry mail exists, to clients or to sales.** `REMINDER_KINDS` is `repo`, `renewal`, `cert`, `eval` (private/account/src/services/reminder.service.ts:127). The `cert` producer `runCertExpiryReminders` (private/account/src/services/reminder.service.ts:314-371) reads `partnerCertificates`, the partner certification, not delegation certs. No producer reads `delegation_certs`.
2. **The delegation-cert queries exist and nothing calls them for mail.** `DelegationCertService.list({expiringWithinDays})` (private/account/src/services/delegation-cert.service.ts:531-548) filters `validUntil < now + N days`; `getHealthStats` (private/account/src/services/delegation-cert.service.ts:617-648) counts active, expiring within 30 days and `expired`.
3. **A superseded cert is `expired`; a lapsed cert stays `active`.** Issuing a new cert marks every earlier `active` cert of the subscription `expired` (private/account/src/services/delegation-cert.service.ts:360-364), and nothing flips the status on time. So "lapsed, no successor, not revoked" is `status = 'active' AND validUntil < now`. A cert carries `subscriptionId` and no org id (private/account/src/db/schema.ts:887-918); the org is reached as the renewal producer reaches it, `subscriptions.customerId` to `organizations.customerId` (private/account/src/services/reminder.service.ts:387-447).
4. **The on-prem box mails only on renewal failure.** `cert-auto-renew.service.ts` sends a failure alert (`consecutive_failures` or `half_validity_elapsed`) and a recovery alert to `ROOT_EMAIL` through `sendAdminAlert` (private/account/src/services/cert-auto-renew.service.ts:276-323), with a per-streak cooldown of 24 hours (private/account/src/services/cert-auto-renew.service.ts:236-243). There is no countdown and no expired alert.
5. **No sales channel exists.** There is no `sales@` and no `SALES_*` in private/account/src. `EmailService.sendAdminAlert` (private/account/src/services/email.service.ts:380-390) mails `env.ROOT_EMAIL`, passed in at construction (private/account/src/services/email.service.ts:168); the env type has `ROOT_EMAIL` (private/account/src/types/env.ts:153).
6. **`ADMIN_EMAIL` is documented and unread.** The secrets comment in private/account/wrangler.toml:74 lists `ADMIN_EMAIL`; no code under private/account/src reads it, and the deploy secret push carries `ROOT_EMAIL` (.ci/rediacc_ci/deploy/set_account_worker_secrets.py:218).
7. **The pieces to reuse.** Owner and admin recipients and a per-recipient fingerprint: `runRenewalReminders` (private/account/src/services/reminder.service.ts:387-447). The notices store kinds (private/account/src/types/notice.ts:10-17). ## Design
DRY: every piece rides the existing reminder, notice, preference and alert pipeline; nothing new is a second path.
- **N1, the `delegation` reminder kind.** `REMINDER_KINDS` gains `delegation`; `runDueReminders` runs `runDelegationCertReminders`, shaped like `runRenewalReminders`. It reads `delegation_certs` with `status = 'active'` and `validUntil` in `(now, now + 30 days]`, resolves the org through the subscription, and mails the org's owners and admins. Thresholds 30, 14, 7 and 1 days: a cert's threshold is the smallest one at or above its days left, and the fingerprint `delegation_expiry:{certId}:{threshold}:{email}` sends one mail per threshold per recipient. Each recipient gets a notice of kind `delegation_cert_expiry` (dedupe key the cert id, severity `warning`, `critical` at 7 days and under; payload `{certId, subscriptionId, planCode, validUntil, daysLeft}`). `NotificationService.sendDelegationCertExpiring`, guarded by `canSendProductOrg(userId, 'delegationExpiring')`, returns `'sent'|'suppressed'|'failed'`, and a failed send is not recorded. The new preference key `delegationExpiring` (default on) goes in private/account/src/types/communication.ts and the defaults in private/account/src/services/communication.service.ts. The template `src/email/templates/delegation-expiry.ts` uses the common layout, and the `delegationExpiry.*` strings go in all 13 account email locales.
- **N2, the sales digest.** `SALES_NOTIFY_EMAIL` (optional email) joins the env type. `EmailService.sendSalesAlert(subject, body)` sits beside `sendAdminAlert` and reuses `sendEmail` and the admin alert HTML; a missing address logs a warning and returns false. `SalesDigestService.run(now)` (new file) builds one mail a day: certs crossing 30 days, certs crossing 7 days, and lapsed certs (N4), each line with the org name, plan, cert id prefix and `validUntil`. Each line is fingerprinted `sales_delegation:{certId}:{30|7|lapsed}` in `notificationReminderLog`, so a cert appears once per stage; an empty digest sends nothing. The 03:00 UTC reminder run in private/account/src/entry/cloudflare.ts calls it after `runDueReminders`, with container wiring. The secret is registered in .ci/config/secret-supply.json (the account profiles that list `ROOT_EMAIL` as optional), .ci/config/bws-secret-map.json and the account secrets push (.ci/rediacc_ci/deploy/set_account_worker_secrets.py).
- **N3, the on-box countdown.** `AutoRenewAlertReason` gains `expiry_countdown` and `cert_expired`. On each check, a loaded cert with 30, 14, 7 or 1 days left sends one countdown alert per threshold, and a cert past `validUntil` sends `cert_expired` once per 24 hours (the existing cooldown), both through `sendAdminAlert` and `renderRenewalFailureEmail`'s template extended for the two reasons. The last countdown threshold sent is kept in the status sidecar so a restart does not resend it. The expired alert text says what degrades (soft degrade, per the ruling).
- **N4, the lapsed query.** `DelegationCertService.listLapsed(now)`: `status = 'active' AND validUntil < now` (revoked and superseded excluded by construction, Context 3), joined to the subscription for plan and customer. N2 consumes it.
- **N5, the portal.** The `delegation_cert_expiry` summary in private/account/web/src/lib/notice-names.ts, and the `delegationExpiring` toggle in private/account/web/src/pages/Notifications.tsx, with the strings in the 13 web locales (`notices.json`, `settings.json`).
- **F1, `ADMIN_EMAIL`: remove the stale comment.** `ROOT_EMAIL` is already the admin alert address in the env type, the secret supply and the deploy push (Context 5, 6); reading `ADMIN_EMAIL` as well would add a second name for one address and a fallback path nobody needs (rule 2). The `ADMIN_EMAIL` line in private/account/wrangler.toml becomes `ROOT_EMAIL, SALES_NOTIFY_EMAIL`. F1 folds into N2.
