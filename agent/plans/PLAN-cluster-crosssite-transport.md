## Ask

Moving a cluster between two independent Ceph clusters (a KVM lab and a Linode site, or two lab sites) is designed in `docs/design/03-fork-attach-snapshots.md` section 4 and refused by `rdc cluster migrate` today. This plan builds the transport and the routing: two renet subcommands that stream an rbd image (full or diff) over stdout and stdin, a CLI pipe that joins them over two SSH sessions, and a cross-site arm of `migrateCluster` chosen by the Ceph fsids, not a flag. It was split out of PLAN-multicluster-local-suite.md by operator ruling on 2026-11-16 so the transport has its own boxes and review; that plan's suite and PLAN-cluster-live-proofs.md's Linode demo are its consumers.

## Design

**D1. The transport is a host-relayed rbd stream.** Two renet subcommands, `renet datastore send --name <ds> --snap <s> [--from-snap <s0>]` (writes `rbd export --export-format 2` or `rbd export-diff` to stdout) and `renet datastore receive --name <ds> --pool <p> --image <i> [--diff]` (reads `rbd import` or `import-diff` from stdin, then verifies the image's snapshot list). The CLI runs both over its own SSH sessions and pipes one into the other, so the two sites never need a route to each other: a NAT-ed lab VM and a public Linode node are both reachable from the operator host. A direct site-to-site push is a later optimization and out of scope. Both subcommands refuse a datastore that is not Ceph-backed. The stream carries the image bytes as stored, so a LUKS datastore crosses as ciphertext, inside SSH.

**D2. One pipe helper, with backpressure.** `pipeExec(src, srcCmd, dst, dstCmd)` in `exec.ts` opens one exec channel per side and pipes the source channel's stdout into the destination channel's stdin with Node stream `pipe` (backpressure, no buffering of the image), returns both exit codes and the byte count, and fails when either side exits non-zero or closes early. `SFTPClient.pipeTo(dst, srcCmd, dstCmd)` wraps it.

**D3. `cluster migrate` routes cross-site by fact, not by flag.** When the dest machine belongs to a cluster whose Ceph fsid differs from the source's (read with `sudo ceph fsid` on one monitor of each), `migrateCluster` hands off to `cluster-migrate-cross-site.ts`; the same fsid keeps the in-Ceph remap. The cross-site path follows the 03 section 4 pipeline across every Ceph datastore of the cluster (`listClusterCephDatastores`): group snap S1 (hot), full stream, up to `N=3` `export-diff` rounds while the source serves (stopping early when a round ships under 64 MiB), then `datastore_adopt` of a record re-pointed at site B's pool and image plus `verifyDatastoreRegistered` for each datastore while the source still serves (Context 4; the split-out draft put adopt after the source down, which repeats the finding #18 order the AS-BUILT block forbids), then `kube_prep_fork` down, final snap, final diff, `datastore_attach`, `kube_identity_rewrite --operation migrate` (CA kept), agent re-join on site B, health gate; the source datastores are detached and kept, and the verb prints the `rdc datastore delete` line for each. `prepareMigrateDest` is not called on the cross-site arm, because site B's members already carry site B's Ceph config and seeding the source's would point them at the wrong cluster. A failure before the source down leaves the source untouched and forgets the adopted records on B; a failure after it re-attaches and restarts the source, as the in-Ceph arm does (Context 4). The local-tier (non-Ceph) refusal stays, reworded to drop "cross-site".

**D4. Contracts.** No FunctionDef is added (the subcommands are raw cobra commands run over SSH, like `renet backup export`) and `cluster migrate` gains no option, so `renet-contract` and `cli-contract` must not drift; their gates are the Accept of the docs box rather than a regeneration step.

**D5. Proof.** Unit tests pin the argv, the call order and the rollback. The live proof of the cross-site leg is PLAN-multicluster-local-suite.md's M4/M6 run on two KVM sites, and the Linode leg is PLAN-cluster-live-proofs.md A4; this plan carries the T0 spike and the hostinger receipt for the renet build.

Out of scope: rbd-mirror as a pre-copy transport, a direct site-to-site push, local-tier (btrfs send / FIEMAP) cross-site transfer, the multi-cluster e2e suite (PLAN-multicluster-local-suite.md) and the Linode demo (PLAN-cluster-live-proofs.md).

## Risks

- **Relay throughput.** The stream passes through the host twice (in from A, out to B). T0 measures it; a pre-copy that runs long only lengthens the run, because downtime tracks the final diff.
- **Image naming on site B.** A record re-pointed at site B's pool must keep the mount path stable (`docs/design/04-cluster-fork-migrate.md:50`, section 2 item 6) so PV objects in kine need no rewrite; T3's test pins it.
- **Record fields that name the source Ceph.** The captured record may carry source-cluster fields beyond pool and image (Context 4); T3 re-points every Ceph field the record holds, and a field T0 or the M6 run finds unmapped is a finding fixed in this plan.
- **fsid read across sites.** A monitor that answers slowly or a dest whose cluster has no Ceph pool must refuse with the reason, never fall back to the in-Ceph arm; T3 pins the refusal.
